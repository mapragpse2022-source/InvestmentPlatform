# 1. Purpose

This document defines the operational governance framework for the Digital Investment Platform.

Its objective is to ensure that daily operations remain standardized, measurable, secure and continuously available.

---

# 2. Operational Principles

Platform operations shall follow these principles:

- Service Availability
- Operational Consistency
- Standardized Procedures
- Accountability
- Automation First
- Continuous Improvement

---

# 3. Operational Objectives

The Operations Team is responsible for ensuring:

- Platform availability
- Service stability
- Infrastructure health
- Operational efficiency
- Customer service continuity
- Incident coordination

---

# 4. Operational Scope

Operational governance applies to:

- Production Infrastructure
- Cloud Resources
- Backend Services
- Databases
- Monitoring Systems
- Customer Support Operations
- Deployment Operations

---

# 5. Operational Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Operations Manager | Overall operational governance |
| DevOps Engineer | Infrastructure and deployments |
| Infrastructure Engineer | Servers and networking |
| Support Team | Customer support operations |
| Security Team | Operational security oversight |
| Executive Management | Operational governance approval |

---

# 6. Governance Principles

Operational decisions should be:

- Documented
- Approved
- Traceable
- Auditable
- Repeatable

Operational procedures should never depend upon undocumented knowledge.

---

# 7. Operational Documentation

Every operational activity should have supporting documentation.

Documentation includes:

- Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
- Runbooks
- Recovery Guides
- Maintenance Procedures
- Deployment Procedures
- Escalation Procedures

---

# 8. Operational Metrics

Operations should continuously monitor:

- Availability
- Uptime
- Incident Count
- Recovery Time
- Change Success Rate
- Customer Impact

Operational metrics should support continuous improvement.

---

# 9. Operational Reviews

Operational governance should include regular management reviews.

Reviews ensure that operational processes remain effective, measurable and aligned with business objectives.

---

## Review Frequency

| Review | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Daily Operations Review | Daily |
| Weekly Operations Meeting | Weekly |
| Monthly Performance Review | Monthly |
| Quarterly Governance Review | Quarterly |
| Annual Operations Assessment | Annually |

Additional reviews may be scheduled following major incidents or significant infrastructure changes.

---

## Review Scope

Operational reviews should evaluate:

- Service Availability
- Operational KPIs
- Infrastructure Health
- Incident Statistics
- Change Success Rate
- Customer Support Performance
- Capacity Planning
- Security Operations

---

# 10. Operational Reporting

Operations should generate standardized reports.

Reports should support technical teams, management and executive stakeholders.

---

## Standard Reports

Examples include:

- Daily Operations Report
- Weekly Infrastructure Report
- Monthly Availability Report
- Incident Summary
- Capacity Report
- SLA Report
- Performance Dashboard

Reports should be retained according to governance policies.

---

## Reporting Principles

Operational reporting should be:

- Accurate
- Timely
- Consistent
- Actionable
- Auditable

Reports should focus on measurable operational outcomes.

---

# 11. Performance Management

Operational performance should be measured continuously.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

Recommended KPIs include:

- Platform Availability
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Recover (MTTR)
- Incident Volume
- Deployment Success Rate
- Customer Ticket Resolution Time
- Infrastructure Utilization
- SLA Compliance

KPIs should be reviewed periodically.

---

## Performance Targets

Performance targets should be realistic, measurable and periodically reviewed.

Failure to meet targets should trigger improvement initiatives.

---

# 12. Operational Documentation Standards

Operational knowledge should be standardized.

The platform should maintain the following documentation types.

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| SOP | Standard Operating Procedure |
| Runbook | Step-by-step operational execution |
| Playbook | Scenario-based operational response |
| Checklist | Validation before or after operational tasks |
| Escalation Matrix | Responsibility and escalation path |

All documentation should remain version controlled.

---

# 13. Continuous Improvement

Operations should continuously improve based on measurable outcomes.

Improvement activities include:

- Root Cause Analysis
- Lessons Learned
- Process Optimization
- Automation Improvements
- Infrastructure Modernization
- Documentation Updates

Operational maturity should increase over time.

---

# 14. AI Implementation Notes

Artificial Intelligence may assist operational teams with monitoring, reporting and documentation.

AI should never independently execute production operational changes.

---

## Approved AI Responsibilities

AI may assist with:

- Operational report generation
- Dashboard summarization
- Trend analysis
- Documentation generation
- Runbook recommendations
- Capacity forecasting

---

## Restricted AI Responsibilities

AI must not:

- Deploy production changes.
- Restart production services.
- Modify infrastructure configuration.
- Override operational approvals.
- Close critical incidents automatically.

---

## Human Responsibility

Final responsibility for operational governance remains with the Operations Team, DevOps Team, Infrastructure Team and Executive Management.

Operational decisions affecting production services require approval from authorized personnel.
