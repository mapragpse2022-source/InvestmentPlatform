# 1. Purpose

This document defines the risk disclosure principles of the Digital Investment Platform.

The purpose is to ensure users understand financial risks, operational risks and platform limitations before participating.

---

# 2. Risk Transparency Principle

The platform must provide clear and understandable information regarding:

- Investment risks
- Market conditions
- Operational limitations
- Digital asset risks
- Financial performance uncertainty

---

# 3. No Guaranteed Outcome

The platform must clearly communicate that investment activities involve risk.

Historical performance or expected performance does not represent a guarantee of future results.

---

# 4. Investment Risk

Users must understand that:

- Investment performance may change.
- Financial results may differ from expectations.
- Capital values may be affected by external factors.

---

# 5. Digital Asset Risk

Because the platform uses blockchain assets, users should understand risks related to:

- Blockchain network issues.
- Transaction delays.
- Wallet security.
- Asset volatility.
- Network fees.

---

# 6. Operational Risk

Operational risks may include:

- Technical failures.
- Service interruptions.
- Blockchain infrastructure issues.
- Security incidents.

The platform must maintain appropriate protection mechanisms.

---

# 7. Loss Handling Policy

The platform must clearly define policies regarding potential losses.

In situations where financial losses occur, any compensation provided by the company is limited according to:

- Internal company policies.
- Approved compensation procedures.
- Applicable legal requirements.

Compensation does not represent a guarantee against investment losses.

---

# 8. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Understanding investment risks.
- Reviewing platform rules.
- Protecting account credentials.
- Providing accurate information.

---

# 9. Risk Acceptance

Before participating in investment activities, users must acknowledge:

- They understand potential risks.
- They have reviewed platform rules.
- They accept investment conditions.

---

# 10. Risk Communication

Risk information must be displayed:

- Before investment creation.
- Before deposit confirmation.
- During account onboarding.

---

# 11. Financial Performance Communication

The platform must distinguish between:

## Expected Performance

A possible future result based on investment rules.

---

## Actual Performance

A recorded financial result.

---

## Paid Amount

A completed settlement amount.

---

# 12. Administrative Responsibility

Authorized administrators must ensure:

- Risk information remains updated.
- Financial rules are documented.
- User communication is accurate.

---

# 13. Security Requirements

Risk-related records should be protected.

The system should maintain:

- User acceptance records.
- Timestamp information.
- Version history of agreements.

---

# 14. Future Compliance Preparation

The architecture should support future requirements:

- Regulatory disclosures.
- Regional compliance rules.
- Investor verification.
- Legal document versioning.

---

# 15. AI Implementation Notes

AI coding assistants must:

- Never remove risk warnings.
- Never present investment outcomes as guaranteed.
- Preserve user acceptance workflows.
- Request documentation updates when financial policies change.
